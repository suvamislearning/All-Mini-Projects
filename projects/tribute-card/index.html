Ada Lovelace

1815 – 1852

The first person to publish an algorithm meant to be run by a machine.

Why she's remembered

  • Wrote notes on Charles Babbage's Analytical Engine that included what's considered the first computer program.
  • Recognized that machines could go beyond calculation, into music and art.
  • Her work stayed largely unread for a century before its significance was understood.
"That brain of mine is something more than merely mortal, as time will show."
